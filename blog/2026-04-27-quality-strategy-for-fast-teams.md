Modern teams move quickly, but quality still needs structure.

<!-- truncate -->

A practical strategy can be built around three layers:

1. Prevention: coding standards, pair reviews, and contract checks.
2. Detection: focused automated test suites and observability.
3. Recovery: fast triage, clear ownership, and rollback readiness.

This model keeps quality work visible, measurable, and connected to real release outcomes.
